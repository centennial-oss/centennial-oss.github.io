Charter

Charitable and educational technology work

Centennial OSS exists to develop, maintain, and distribute free and open-source software for the general public. The organization promotes accessible, privacy-respecting, user-focused technology.

Its educational work includes creating and sharing software tools that support productivity, creativity, and learning, while preserving those tools for long-term public benefit.

Commitments enshrined in our articles of incorporation

✓ Public Access

Software and related resources are made available to the public without charge and without advertising.

✓ No Monetization

The corporation does not sell, fee-license, or monetize its software or its users' personal data.

✓ Data Restraint

Personal data is not collected, tracked, or retained except where necessary for voluntary donations or legal, tax, and regulatory obligations.

✓ Nonprofit Limits

No earnings benefit private persons, and assets are dedicated to 501(c)(3)-aligned public purposes on dissolution.
