Privacy policy

Centennial OSS apps do not collect, transmit, or share personal data.

Last updated: April 29, 2026. This policy covers MIDI Scribe, Clipboard Envy, Consolation (macOS / iPad), Consolation (Android), and future Centennial OSS apps unless an app-specific policy says otherwise.

Summary

We collect nothing. Our apps are built to keep app data on your device and to prohibit advertising, analytics, tracking, profiling, and external server communication.

Data collection

Centennial OSS apps do not:

  • Collect personal information.
  • Transmit app data over the internet.
  • Use analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking.
  • Communicate with external servers.

The current apps have no network entitlements and cannot make or accept network connections.

Data storage

Clipboard Envy stores snippets and app settings locally on your Mac.

Consolation stores feature preferences, such as window and volume settings, locally. Audio and video from connected capture devices are transient while viewing and are not recorded, streamed, saved, analyzed, or transmitted by the app.

MIDI Scribe stores practice take data and settings in your private storage locally on your device.

Policy changes

We may update this policy occasionally. We will not update it to permit advertising, profiling, snooping, or analytics collection.

Contact

Questions can be sent to privacy@centennialoss.org. For app-specific issues, use the public GitHub issue trackers for MIDI Scribe, Clipboard Envy, Consolation (macOS / iPad), or Consolation (Android).
